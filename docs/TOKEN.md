## Allocation

| Share | Who | Terms |
|---|---|---|
| **92%** | Open market | Launched on Pons; anyone could buy from the first second at a set starting price. At graduation 28.6% of supply was set aside: about 20.4% seeds the permanent liquidity pool and 8.16% goes into Pons' own locker for good. Both sit in contracts without a withdrawal function |
| **8%** | Treasury | Bought at launch with our own ETH. 7.4% is locked in a public vesting contract that releases it evenly over 12 months into the treasury's cold wallet; 0.6% is unlocked from launch |

The 8% is the only allocation. It also pays the team. There is no private sale and no advisor tranche.

## Trading fee

Every ONAGI buy and sell pays 3% in total: Pons' 1% base fee plus Onagi's 2% creator fee. 2.7% goes to the Onagi fee pool and 0.3% stays with Pons.

## Buyback and burn

25% of the fee pool funds open-market purchases followed by burns.

- **Its own wallet, its own key.** ETH accumulates in the published buyback wallet, apart from the treasury.
- **Hourly and automatic.** A scheduled run buys with the ETH that has built up. Nobody starts it by hand.
- **Small and regular.** At most five purchases of up to 0.02 ETH each per run, with a 1% slippage limit, to limit price impact and sniping.
- **Burned, not held.** Every token bought goes to the burn address.
- **Public.** Every purchase and every burn is an on-chain transaction.

## Disclosure

ONAGI is not a share. Holding it grants no right to revenue, no claim on the treasury and no control over the platform. Rewards depend on platform fees and can change.
