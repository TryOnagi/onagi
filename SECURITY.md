# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you find a security issue in Onagi (the web app, the Telegram agent, the API or any public wallet or contract), please report it privately to **info@onagi.si** with "Security" in the subject.

Please include:

- What you found and where
- Steps to reproduce
- The impact you believe it has

Please do not disclose the issue publicly, or test it against other users' accounts or funds, until we have replied and fixed it. We will acknowledge your report and keep you updated until it is resolved.

## Scope

- https://onagi.si and https://app.onagi.si
- The Telegram agent [@OnagiAgentBot](https://t.me/OnagiAgentBot)
- The public wallets and contracts listed in [docs/VERIFICATION.md](docs/VERIFICATION.md)

Issues in third-party venues (Lighter, Polymarket, Pons, Uniswap) or in Privy should be reported to those teams directly.

## Never share your keys

Onagi will never ask for your recovery phrase, private key or Telegram link code. Anyone who asks is a scammer.

More about how user funds are protected: [docs/SECURITY-MODEL.md](docs/SECURITY-MODEL.md)
